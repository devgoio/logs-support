Logs Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Files on your Mac

Logs by DevGo reads the files you select. Indexing, timestamp detection, searches, and grouping run on your Mac. Logs keeps your workspace, preferences, and bookmarks locally. Bookmarks can contain short excerpts. Editing a document creates a backup. DevGo does not receive your files when you open them.

Optional AI analysis

If you request AI analysis, Logs shows the excerpt and the configured provider before sending it. The request sends the displayed excerpt, line numbers, level labels, and instructions to your chosen endpoint. Basic redaction masks common secrets and email addresses. It can miss sensitive information. Review the preview before sending. Your provider receives the request, uses your key for authentication, and handles the data under its own terms and retention policy. Contact that provider about deleting data it received.

Remote connections

When you connect to a server, your Mac sends the connection details and credentials required by that server and downloads a read-only copy into its local cache. FTP transmits data in plain text. AI keys are stored in macOS Keychain. You can remove a provider key by clearing it in AI Settings and saving.

Tracking and support

The app has no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. If you email DevGo, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. You can ask about or request deletion of a support message at lm@usev.cn.

Logs 隐私政策（简体中文）

本机文件

Logs 在 Mac 上读取你选定的文件，完成索引、时间识别、搜索和分组。工作区、偏好设置和可能含有少量原文的书签保存在本机。编辑时会创建备份。打开文件不会把内容发送给 DevGo。

可选的 AI 解读

主动请求 AI 解读时，Logs 会把预览中的日志片段、行号、级别和分析指令发到你配置的服务商。基础脱敏可能遗漏敏感内容，请先检查预览。服务商使用你的密钥验证身份，并按自己的政策处理、留存或删除数据。有关已发送内容的删除请求，请联系该服务商。

远程连接与密钥

连接服务器时，Mac 会向该服务器提供连接所需的信息和凭据，并将只读副本下载到本地缓存。FTP 使用明文传输。AI 密钥保存在 macOS 钥匙串中，在 AI 设置中清空对应密钥并保存即可删除。

跟踪与联系

应用没有广告、跟踪或第三方分析 SDK。向 DevGo 发送支持邮件时，你的邮箱和来信用于处理问题。如需查询或删除支持邮件，请联系 lm@usev.cn。
